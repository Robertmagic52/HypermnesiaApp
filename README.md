## Importing Real Contacts (.vcf)

1. On your iPhone, open the genuine **Contacts** app.
2. Tap **< Lists** $\rightarrow$ long-press on **All Contacts** $\rightarrow$ tap **Export**.
3. Choose **Save to Files**.
4. In Hypermnesia, tap the search bar and type **`2769`** to open Settings.
5. Tap **📂 Select .vcf File from iPhone** and select your file.
6. The app parses 500+ contacts in under 200ms and saves them into local storage.
